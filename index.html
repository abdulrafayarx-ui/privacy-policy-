Climb the Leaderboard

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026 · https://climbtheleaderboard.site/privacy.html

Climb the Leaderboard (“the app”, “we”) is a school motivation product. It turns verified schoolwork into XP, levels, and class leaderboards. This policy explains what we collect and why. It applies to the website at climbtheleaderboard.site and the Android app (package site.climbtheleaderboard.app).

1. Who this is for

The app is used by a school: students, teachers, and a school admin. It is not a consumer social network. It is not for advertising or selling student data.

2. Information we collect

We store only what is needed to run a class:

  • Account email (Google sign-in or email sign-in)
  • Name, username, optional profile photo
  • Role (student, teacher, or admin), class, and section
  • School activity: assignments, quizzes, challenges, XP, levels, streaks, badges, shop cosmetics
  • Work photos that a student submits for teacher review
  • Support messages and in-app feedback you send
  • On the Android app, camera access is used only when you choose to photograph schoolwork

We do not collect your Google password. Session tokens stay on your device.

3. How we use it

  • Sign you in and keep you in the correct class portal
  • Show class leaderboards, XP, levels, and achievements
  • Let teachers review work and award verified XP
  • Let school admin manage classes, students, and teachers
  • Respond to support and feedback

Students cannot change XP, rank, level, or badges themselves. Opening the app does not earn XP. We do not use schoolwork records for ads.

4. Who can see it

  • You can see your own profile and progress
  • Classmates can see names and XP on that class leaderboard
  • Teachers in the class can review submissions and award XP
  • School admin can see school-wide student and teacher records needed to run the product

5. Sign-in and processors

Sign-in and database hosting use Google (if you choose Continue with Google) and Supabase. Those services process account data under their own policies so the app can authenticate you and store class records.

6. Children and school accounts

Accounts are created for a school class. A school admin or teacher should only add students who are allowed to use the product under school rules. We do not sell children’s data. We do not show third-party advertising in the app.

7. Retention and deletion

Records stay while the school uses the product. If you want an account removed, ask your teacher or school admin, or email us. We will delete the linked profile and class records from the project database when a valid request is made.

8. Security

Access is limited by role. Class portals use a school password. XP is awarded only after teacher approval or a verified system event. We do not let students self-award XP.
